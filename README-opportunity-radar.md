# Opportunity Radar — initial skill proposal

A **read-only, evidence-first discovery agent** for self-service B2C digital products (web utilities, micro-SaaS and apps). Budget: EUR 100–150. Goal: evaluate a plausible path to EUR 10k/month in **revenue**, without outbound B2B sales.

See [SKILL.md](skills/opportunity-radar/SKILL.md). Source skills are linked in the routing table, not blindly installed. Some require API keys, paid external services or a specific runtime. No third-party dependencies are executed by this proposal.

## Current status
Research architecture and skill routing ONLY. No live market validation, installer audit, product implementation or continuous monitoring has been performed. All opportunity results require a research run and source verification.

## Suggested first run
> Scan 20 self-serve B2C web/app opportunities, validate 3 using independent observed signals, produce scenarios to EUR 10k monthly gross revenue and recommend a single EUR 20-or-less experiment. Never invent market numbers and do not spend or publish without approval.

## Suggested validation fixtures
- Negative: an idea with high trend interest but no intent to pay
- Negative: a crowded market with no clear differentiation
- Negative: an app requiring developer fees and multiple paid data providers outside EUR 150
- Positive: a narrowly scoped web utility with demonstrated pain, long-tail queries and feasible free distribution
- Data unavailable: mark unknown rather than assuming 0 or inventing a revenue estimate
