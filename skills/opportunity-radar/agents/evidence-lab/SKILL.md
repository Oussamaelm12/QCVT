# Evidence Lab
Only enter after a subject has been **explicitly selected**. Exclude all user profile, prior projects and personal history. Ask: what exact problem, whose budget, how often, what workaround, how much lost, why now, how found, who regulates it?

## Search sequence
1. State market/geography and a falsifiable research question; search original regulators, official stats and standards first.
2. Find the actual buyer and job-to-be-done through *independently attributable* communities, user reviews, interviews and direct observations; do not extrapolate frequencies from biased samples.
3. Compare direct alternatives and substitutes; inspect their **official** pricing/features, trial limits, support terms, integration limitations and public complaints.
4. Check addressable demand and channel intent; separate searches, estimated visits, relative Google Trends scores, real usage and actual payments.
5. Map technical ecosystem: official APIs, GitHub maintained libraries, skills/MCP connectors, integration permissions, licenses, deployment constraints, security and dependencies.
6. Check regulatory, privacy, intellectual-property, platform-policy and accessibility concerns where materially relevant.
7. Run contradiction search: evidence that the problem is already solved, weak, seasonal, too small, hard to monetize or impossible to acquire cheaply.
8. Stop unbounded research when additional evidence will not alter the next buying/test decision. Track unresolved gaps.

## Output
Source register (URL, **date of original**, access date, author/publisher, geography, primary/secondary, legal access, confidence, duplicate lineage), claim ledger (observed/inferred/hypothesis/contradicted), customer segments and jobs, competitors/substitutes, prices if directly checked, measurable pain, distribution, risks, unknowns, 5–10 prioritized follow-up questions.
**Never** say a URL is verified unless inspected; do not pretend multi-domain content is independent.
Create structured `sources`, `findings`, `competitors` fields described in ../../VENTURE-STUDIO.md.
No expenses, scraping against terms, account changes or outreach without permission.

## Mandatory innovation groundwork
Run benchmark on at least three direct competitors or substitutes with matched attributes (official pricing, core jobs, onboarding, integrations, complaints, support and constraints), marking unavailable data UNKNOWN. Capture actual source links and review status. Use JTBD and Design Thinking **empathize/define** to establish the customer's desired progress and observable struggle; do not simulate interviews as real. Deliver `innovation_engine.py` source, competitor and JTBD records for Strategy Lab.
